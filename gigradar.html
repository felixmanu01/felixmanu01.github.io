Gig Radar Stockholm.

Gig Radar combines concert data I scraped from Debaser with live music data from two external APIs. The project explores how separate data sources can be combined to find artist discovery signals and give marketing decisions a stronger foundation.

Ready to analyse the first eight events

The pipeline

Concert data

Upcoming shows are loaded from my own Debaser scraping result, with artist, date, venue and genre.

MusicBrainz

Each artist is searched in MusicBrainz to collect a live match signal and artist information.

iTunes Search

A second live lookup checks whether the artist can also be found in the iTunes music catalogue.

The research score is an experimental signal made for this case. A higher value means the artist was less consistently identified across the two services. It's meant to show which artists need more research first, and it doesn't predict ticket sales or campaign performance.

How it works

Scraped source

A Node script collects public concert information from Debaser and saves the result as a text file inside the portfolio project.

scraping/scraped_data.txt

API mashup

Artist names from the scraped dataset are searched in MusicBrainz and iTunes. The results are then combined with the original concert information in the browser.

Debaser data + MusicBrainz + iTunes

Business question

Can outside discovery signals make a long concert list easier to prioritise before deeper audience and campaign research begins?

Measurement

The page sends custom events to the dataLayer when an analysis starts and when it finishes. They are set up as triggers in Google Tag Manager and analysed in GA4.
