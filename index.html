Aroma Roast

Aroma Roast sells decaf coffee. The brand was talking broadly to women aged 30 to 40, with no reason for anyone to act. We pointed the whole campaign at one moment instead: pregnancy, when coffee disappears from the morning routine overnight and the ritual is still missed. Targeting 28 to 35 year olds, we built a full funnel on Meta with one job per stage, from recognition to purchase. I shot and produced all of the photography. The campaign drove real sales and brought in more than it spent.

Kungliga Operan

The brief was to make the case for why opera and ballet still matter. The Royal Opera's own audience data pointed to the real problem: a third of Swedes aged 18 to 84 want to visit but never do. The barrier was perception more than price. We built the campaign concept "Gåshud för 65 kr" ("Goosebumps for 65 SEK"), translating the physical sensations of live performance (a racing pulse, dilated pupils, goosebumps) into five outdoor posters, each pairing a macro shot of the body's reaction with the actual ticket price. We planned the rollout across Stockholm's everyday neighbourhoods, not just the city centre, to show that the experience belongs to everyone.

About

Growth marketing sits between creativity, psychology, strategy and numbers. I like it because one campaign uses all four.

I'm Felix Manu, a growth marketer based in Stockholm. My focus is performance marketing: paid social and search, measured all the way to the sale.

Before marketing I made content for TikTok and Instagram, so I know what it takes to stop someone mid-scroll. Today I put that to work in ads, and I want to know why one worked and how the next one can do better.

I've also run my own e-commerce store on Shopify and marketed it with Meta ads, so I know the whole chain from product page to checkout.

My education is in Growth Marketing at Berghs School of Communication, where I've run paid campaigns, built data cases and worked on briefs for brands like Kungliga Operan.
