Stockholm Marathon content plan.

The goal of the plan is to make one data insight easy to understand in several formats, without changing what the data actually shows.

Type
Content strategy
Channels
LinkedIn, video, press, web
Built on
The heat analysis
Core insight

6.3 percent drop out in hot editions, against 3.4 percent in cool ones.

Four formats, one message

LinkedIn

A short post with one clear number, a chart and a link to the full analysis.

Short video

Thirty seconds. Opens with the 12:00 start time and goes straight to the contrast between 6.3 and 3.4 percent.

Press pitch

A pitch written for journalists, with the main finding, the method and a link to the data.

Web

The full analysis with data, sources and limitations.

Communication principle

Same core

The message stays the same in every channel. The core insight doesn't change when the content is adapted to a new format.

Different form

What changes between channels is the form, the pace and the level of detail. The analysis describes a correlation, and every format says so.

In my Stockholm Marathon case I compared six editions and found that the share of runners who drop out is higher in the hot races in my dataset.

This is a correlation in the material. It doesn't prove that heat alone makes every runner stop.
