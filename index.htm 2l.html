Case 01 — Strategic Marketing · Team Project

Kungliga Operan

Working in a team, the brief was to bring a new audience segment to Kungliga Operan (The Royal Swedish Opera) — people who don't yet see opera as something for them. We built the campaign concept “Gåshud för 65 kr” (“Goosebumps for 65 SEK”), reframing the emotional peak of a live performance as a low-cost, low-commitment first experience — designed to lower the barrier to walking through the door.
